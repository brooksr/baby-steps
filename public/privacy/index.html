BabySteps

Privacy Policy

Effective September 4, 2026

BabySteps is an offline-first baby-care log. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information you provide

You may enter a baby profile, care events, notes, caregiver reference information, and settings. This can include sensitive health-related information. Only enter information you are authorized to manage and share.

Where information is stored

BabySteps stores data locally in your browser for offline use. If you connect Google Sheets, the app reads and writes tracker data in the configured shared spreadsheet using your Google account authorization. BabySteps does not operate a separate application server for your care data.

Google account access

Google sign-in and Sheets access are used only to provide the synchronization features you request. Google account data is not sold or used for advertising. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Sharing and retention

The app does not sell personal information. Anyone who can access the connected spreadsheet may be able to view or edit its contents. Local data remains on the device until browser storage is cleared. Synced data remains in Google Sheets until you delete it there or through the app.

Your choices

You can use the app locally, export your data, delete entries, clear browser storage, or revoke BabySteps access from your Google Account permissions. Revoking access prevents future synchronization but does not delete the spreadsheet.

Security and children

Reasonable safeguards are used, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. BabySteps is intended for use by adult caregivers and is not directed to children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The effective date above will be revised when that happens. Questions can be sent to the BabySteps project maintainer through the channel where you received access to the app.
